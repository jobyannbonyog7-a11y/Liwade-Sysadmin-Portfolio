A sysadmin keeps

System administration is the work of installing, configuring, securing, and maintaining the computers, servers, and networks that people and organizations depend on every day.

Explore the role Try the Linux terminal

What does a system administrator do?

Think of a sysadmin as the caretaker of an organization's technology. When everything works, nobody notices. That is the goal: reliable systems, protected data, and people who can do their work without interruption.
